Dynamic analysis Optional

Measure the rider's joint angles while pedalling, from a side-view video or a live camera.

  • Bike on a trainer, filmed from the side the rider is facing
  • Camera level with the hip, 3–4 m away, whole bike in frame
  • 15–20 s at a steady, normal cadence
  • Tight clothing and good light help the tracking
Video is processed on this device and never uploaded. Only the angles and two still frames are kept.
